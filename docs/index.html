Dimensional Weight Test Vectors

A small, reproducible reference for checking parcel dimensional-weight implementations. It separates unit systems, divisors, rounding and billable-weight selection so results can be compared without assuming a shipping price.

Metric formula

Use centimetres for all three dimensions and kilograms for actual weight.

dim_kg = (length_cm × width_cm × height_cm) ÷ divisor

Imperial formula

Use inches for all three dimensions and pounds for actual weight.

dim_lb = (length_in × width_in × height_in) ÷ divisor

Billable weight

For these test vectors, select the greater of actual and dimensional weight, then round the displayed result to two decimal places.

billable = max(actual, dimensional)

Implementation notes

  • Do not mix centimetres with an imperial divisor or inches with a metric divisor.
  • Keep the unrounded dimensional value for the comparison step.
  • Apply carrier-specific rounding or minimums separately from this reference.
  • Record packed dimensions, not an assumed product-box size.

What this reference does not claim

A matching calculation does not verify a carrier tariff, destination fee, parcel acceptance, customs outcome or delivery time. Divisors and billing rules can vary by service and date.
