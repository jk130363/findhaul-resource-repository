# FindHaul Research Resources

A versioned collection of practical checklists for reviewing shopping-agent links, spreadsheet references, and QC-photo evidence. It is maintained alongside [FindHaul](https://findhaul.com/), an independent research and discovery resource. FindHaul does not sell products, process payments, or guarantee sellers, listings, prices, or shipping outcomes.

## Spreadsheet and link verification

- Record the original publisher, source URL, and review date.
- Inspect the destination domain before signing in or paying.
- Match the item identifier, option, size, and color on the seller page.
- Treat redirects, changed domains, and missing source rows as review flags.
- Verify current price, stock, agent fees, shipping terms, and restrictions separately.
- Do not infer authenticity, seller quality, or safety from inclusion in a spreadsheet.

## QC evidence review

- Confirm that photos belong to the exact item or order under review, not a promotional example.
- Record the image source and observation date.
- Match the visible variant, size label, measurements, and requested details.
- Note missing angles, unreadable labels, and unresolved inconsistencies.
- Treat the presence of QC photos as evidence to inspect, not proof of quality or authenticity.

## Agent comparison notes

- Compare like-for-like services and use dated first-party sources where possible.
- Separate product price, service fee, payment fee, domestic shipping, international shipping, storage, and optional services.
- Mark unavailable information as unknown; do not convert missing data into a zero or a ranking.

For the full consumer checklist, visit the [FindHaul spreadsheet link-safety guide](https://findhaul.com/spreadsheet-link-safety).

## Corrections

Open an issue with the affected URL, what changed, and the date observed. Do not post order numbers, addresses, account details, or other personal information.
